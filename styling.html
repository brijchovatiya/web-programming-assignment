About Me

At first, I thought CSS was only for making a page look colorful. I am starting to understand that the more important job of CSS is making the page comfortable to read and keeping everything organized.

The main pages use the same CSS file. That means I can change the header color once and the change appears across the website, instead of editing every page separately.

I kept this website simple on purpose. I wanted it to look like something I could understand and rebuild myself, while still showing that I know how to use different fonts, colors, and paragraph styles.
